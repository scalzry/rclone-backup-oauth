Privacy policy

rclone backup is used only by its owner, to store the owner's own encrypted backups in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Data accessed: only files this app creates in the owner's Google Drive (drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files.
  • Use: uploading, listing, downloading and deleting those backup files on the owner's behalf.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to or sent to anyone else.
  • Storage: the Google sign-in token stays on the owner's own server. Backup contents are encrypted before upload.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the Google account's security settings.
